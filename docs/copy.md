# Site copy

Copy is part of the work, not filler around it. German is the reference, English follows.

## Voice

- Informal "Du". Plain words and short sentences, without thinning the substance. No agency phrases ("Frist im Nacken"), no slogans ("Lösungen statt Listen").
- No bragging, nothing robotic. Say what happens instead of "Ich finde …", and read like
  a person talking.
- Phrase it positively. "Keine Lust auf Felder?" starts from the negative.
- No em dashes. Colons, commas and full stops carry the rhythm.
- Short imperatives, as people speak: "Drück", "Erzähl", "Schreib". Where the short form
  sounds wrong, the long one stays ("Ergänze").
- Say a thing once. The same argument in two sections reads as padding.
- Text before layout: write the copy, then build the element around it.

## Claims and terms

- No invented numbers or studies. Either it is sourced or it does not appear. Sourced
  figures are explained in a sentence, never stacked, and the source is named or linked.
- No invented tool behaviour: screen reader announcements, Figma, DevTools. Check first.
- Terminology has to be right. WCAG has success criteria, not paragraphs. EN 301 549
  V4.1.1 (September 2026) references WCAG 2.2, which this project tests against. Until it is
  cited in the EU Official Journal, no version grants presumption of conformity under the
  EAA and the BFSG: V3.2.1 (WCAG 2.1) is cited only for public bodies. The site therefore
  calls EN 301 549 and WCAG the benchmark "in practice" and makes no claim about the legal
  status. Public bodies (BITV) are not part of the offer and are not addressed.
- Read every sentence for what it literally claims. Grammar that parses is not a sentence
  that means the intended thing.
- Correct German: "in der Datenschutzerklärung", not "im Datenschutz".

## Fixed wording

- Buttons say what happens: "Projekt anfragen", "Zurück", "Weiter", "Zur Ansage".
- The inquiry: never "Vorhaben"; the page asks "Worum geht es?" and says "Dein Projekt".
- The first call is "unverbindlich und kostenlos", never only "kostenlos".
  In English: "free, with no strings attached".
- An example is marked as one: "Jedes Projekt ist anders, deshalb stimmen wir den Ablauf auf
  dich ab. So kann er zum Beispiel … aussehen."
- Error messages are friendly and clear, without control terms ("Haken raus").
- An empty state says nothing has happened yet and what one can do, never "Bereit".

## English

Natural American English, no em dashes, no AI filler phrases. Screen reader transcripts
are not translations: English VoiceOver says `button` and `edit text` where the German
voice says `Taste` and `Textfeld`.
